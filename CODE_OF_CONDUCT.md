# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be welcoming, practical and safe to take part in, whatever your background, experience level, country or role. It is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standards

Expected:
- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the project, and keep other people's company information out of it. Never post real company data, secrets, tokens or personal identifiers in issues, pull requests or discussions, yours or anyone else's.
- Credit others' work.

Not acceptable:
- Harassment, insults, slurs, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language, or sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission, including real company or financial details.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained unwelcome contact.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in the repository (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, commit messages) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns privately through [a private security advisory](https://github.com/arunmenon1807/ai-management-office/security/advisories/new) and start the title with "Conduct:". Do not open a public issue. Reports are handled by the maintainer, kept confidential, and reviewed promptly and fairly. If the report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's own abuse reporting for the repository.

Consequences, depending on severity and history: a private request to stop, a public warning, removal of comments, a temporary block, or a permanent ban from the project.
